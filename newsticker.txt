-=- War News -=-
Federate counter-pushes intensify as the Five-Day Strategic Liberation Plan enters active execution across multiple sectors.
The battle for Drejdelo proper reignites; fighting inside and around the city grows heavier as Federate forces attempt to restore nominal power access to affected regions.
Remnadian refugees fleeing Drejdelo warn that insurgent commanders may be preparing a hostage crisis if defensive positions continue collapsing.
Oestinian territorial pockets remain under severe pressure; military observers warn that a collapse of the Oestinian front could dramatically prolong the war.
The Federation announces the beginning of implementation measures for the new administrative subdivision known as the Federate Sector of Remnadia.
A large-scale terrorist attack plot targeting Allos and federal leadership structures is prevented after expedited anti-terror operations.
Minister of Internal Security Valten says: “The enemies of the Federation sought fear and paralysis. They achieved neither.”
A senior High Command official regarding the Five-Day Plan: “This operation was not designed to prolong the war, it was designed to end it and we strive to make the best decisions for the people and for safety.”

-=- New in the Federation -=-
A minor revision to the new Federate flag centers the national symbol following governmental review and public discussion.
Some Oestinian soldiers mistakenly exchange fire with Federate units after incorrectly identifying their position as insurgent territory; the incident is rapidly deconflicted.
In several reconquered Remnadian towns, women perform traditional dances while waving Federate flags; soldiers join celebrations and spontaneous festivals form in secured areas.
Military convoys, STRACON logistics traffic, and reserve deployments increase visibly throughout Aestovia and Plaetonia.
Churches and public squares across major Federate cities host vigils following the attempted attack in Allos.
Local businesses report increased purchases of emergency goods as households continue adapting to wartime conditions.
