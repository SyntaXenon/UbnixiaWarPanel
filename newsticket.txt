-=- War News -=-

The Drejdelo Front remains highly active; territorial progress remains limited but pressure on insurgent positions intensifies.
The Federation announces a Five-Day Strategic Liberation Plan aimed at the decisive defeat of the Remnadian Insurgency.
Military coordination between the Federation and Oestinia strengthens significantly amid joint anti-rebel efforts.
Oestinian positions continue to suffer severe operational strain as insurgent advances threaten multiple strategic sectors.
Large Oestinian territorial pockets are reportedly approaching full encirclement and siege conditions after continued insurgent pressure.
Remnadian positions in Oestinia face intensified combat as Federate and Oestinian commands attempt to prevent additional territorial isolation.
Military presence escalates throughout the Federate Electoral Dominions of Aestovia and Plaetonia as security measures expand.
High Command reports improving command coordination following the recent strategic calamities.
Local oil shortage remains imminent but emergency stockpile distribution has stabilized some vulnerable areas.
Magistrate approves expanded wartime operations after receiving neither Senate veto nor common rejection.

Federate families continue adapting to emergency power measures as Drejdelo restoration operations remain underway.
